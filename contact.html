FAQ

Common questions

Does my child need their own laptop?

A limited number of laptops are available for physical Saturday classes; for online modules, any laptop or desktop with internet access works.

Can my child switch between online and physical classes?

Yes. Both formats follow the same curriculum, so learners can move between them as their schedule changes.

What if my child has no coding experience at all?

That's the norm, not the exception. The programme starts with IT basics before any coding is introduced.

How do I track my child's progress?

Once registered, your child can create a learner profile and log in to see module progress on their personal dashboard.
