Curriculum & classes

What we teach, and how classes work

A five-module path from IT fundamentals to real programming, delivered through Saturday classes and always-on online modules.

The curriculum

Five modules, one continuous path

Learners move through modules in order. Each one includes guided lessons, practice exercises and a small project.

🖥️

IT & Internet Basics

How computers work, files and folders, using the internet safely, and everyday typing and software skills.

Module 1
📄

HTML Foundations

Structuring a web page: headings, paragraphs, links, images and lists — the building blocks of every website.

Module 2
🎨

CSS Styling

Colour, fonts, spacing and layout — learners restyle their HTML pages and make them their own.

Module 3
⚡

JavaScript Logic

Variables, conditions, loops and events. Buttons that respond, simple games, and interactive pages.

Module 4
🐍

Intro to Python

A second language that reinforces core programming ideas: input, output, loops and simple functions.

Module 5
🏆

Capstone project

Learners bring modules together into one project of their choice — a personal site, a small game, or a script.

Final project
Class formats

Choose the format that fits your family

🏫

Physical classes

Saturdays, 10:00–12:00, at our Johannesburg venue. Best for learners who enjoy in-person guidance and working alongside other kids.

  • Small group sizes
  • Instructor on hand for every exercise
  • Laptops available on site, or bring your own
🌐

Online classes

Modules are uploaded online and can be worked through any time, at a pace that suits your child's schedule.

  • Learn from any location
  • Revisit lessons as often as needed
  • Progress tracked on your child's learner profile
Who it's for

Built specifically for ages 10–16

Lessons, pacing and language are all calibrated for this age group — challenging enough to hold attention, simple enough to build real confidence.

No experience needed

We start from the fundamentals, so a first-time learner and a curious tinkerer both have room to grow.

Progress at their pace

Online modules can be repeated; physical classes reinforce whatever a learner is working through.

Something to show for it

Every module ends in a small, shareable project — a page, a script, a game.
